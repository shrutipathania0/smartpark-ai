SmartPark AI Multi-Stage Computer Vision Pipeline

1. YOLOv8 Detection
Custom lightweight PyTorch model trained on authentic Indian vehicles extracts high-precision plate bounding boxes (mAP@50: 91.2%).
2. OpenCV Preprocessing
Bilateral noise reduction, CLAHE contrast enhancement, and adaptive thresholding for clear character separation.
3. OCR & Disambiguation
EasyOCR character recognition with Indian RTO position-aware character disambiguation (O/0, I/1, Z/2, S/5, B/8).
4. Deterministic Allocation
Atomic database locking guarantees strict category isolation across Disabled, EV Charging, and Normal parking.
